Tangible Interactions for Playing

A new play experience joining LEGO building with digital elements, designed in close contact with children at LEGO Concept Lab.

Organisation
LEGO System A/S · Concept Lab, Billund
Year
Jan – Jun 2009
Role
Designer, new play experiences
Disciplines
Child-centred design · Contextmapping · Tangible interaction · AR prototyping

Challenge

Concept Lab asked for a toy that integrates LEGO's physical building with new digital elements, for boys aged 5 to 11, with the 2011 toy market in view.

Approach

A child-centred, vision-led process. Contextmapping research with parents as co-researchers, then four play iterations with children, building every prototype in LEGO.

Outcome

A small-scale LEGO city whose plates carry augmented reality markers, seen through a LEGO-built handheld viewer. Children grasped that a physical element can stand for a virtual one and moved markers freely between scenes.

Commissioned project undertaken through the TU Delft graduation programme (MSc Design for Interaction, 2009). The full thesis is publicly available in the TU Delft repository [link to add].
